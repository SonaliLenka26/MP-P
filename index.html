🍝 Creamy Veg Pasta

Here's my all-time favourite recipe!

This is my favourite dish! It is quick, tasty and easy to make. Ready in only 25 minutes.

🛒 Ingredients

  • 200gm pasta
  • 1 onion, chopped
  • 1 tomato, chopped
  • 1 carrot and 1/2 capsicum, chopped
  • 1 cup milk
  • 2 tbsp butter
  • Salt, pepper and mixed herbs

👩‍🍳 Instructions:

  1. Boil the pasta in salt water.
  2. In a pan, melt butter and fry the onion until golden.
  3. Add the vegetables and cook for 2-3 minutes.
  4. Add milk to it and wait for it to boil.
  5. Stir the salt, pepper and mixed herbs.
  6. Drain the pasta and mix it with the sauce.
